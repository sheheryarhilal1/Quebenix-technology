Project Overview

Simplifying Business Sales and Operations.

Traditional sales management often involves manual billing, time-consuming calculations, and scattered product and transaction records. These challenges can make it difficult for businesses to manage daily sales efficiently and maintain organized business information.

This POS System provides a digital solution for handling sales transactions, organizing product information, and simplifying billing workflows. The application is designed to make everyday business tasks more convenient through a clear and user-friendly interface.

Project Type Point of Sale System
Platform Android / Mobile
Development Flutter & Dart
The Challenge

Making daily sales, billing, and product management simpler for businesses.

01

Problem

Manual billing and sales recording can slow down transactions and increase the chances of calculation errors. Managing product details, reviewing previous transactions, and maintaining organized records can also become difficult when business information is handled manually.

02

Solution

I developed a mobile-based POS application using Flutter to provide an organized sales workflow. The application focuses on simplifying billing, supporting product handling, and making sales-related information easier to manage through a user-friendly interface.

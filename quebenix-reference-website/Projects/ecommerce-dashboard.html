Project Overview

Turning ecommerce data into business insights.

Ecommerce businesses generate large amounts of information from orders, customers, products, payments and sales. Without an interactive dashboard, it can be difficult to understand overall business performance.

This project was developed to organize ecommerce data into clear KPIs and interactive visualizations, allowing users to quickly monitor sales, orders, customers and product performance.

Dataset Ecommerce Sales Data
Analysis Sales & Customer Analytics
Output Interactive Power BI Dashboard
The Challenge

From raw ecommerce data to clear insights.

01

Problem

The business data contained multiple records related to orders, products, customers and sales. It was difficult to quickly identify sales trends, order performance, customer behavior and the products contributing most to business results.

02

Solution

I cleaned and transformed the ecommerce data and created an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs, charts and filters to make sales performance easier to monitor and analyze.
